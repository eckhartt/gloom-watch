## The ruling

Three prototypes were built and **rejected as designs**. Only the owner's
reaction carries forward:

- **The binder view is the app** — a visual grid of every variant where owned vs
  needed is obvious at a glance.
- **"The Gap" is a filter over the binder, never its own screen.**
- **Tapping a card opens a bottom sheet**, keeping the binder as persistent
  context.
- **No aggregate density map** above the grid.
- **Visual style: dense, precise, typographic** — the "Ledger" direction, not
  ornamental.

Deliberately nothing more was recorded. The prototypes were not good enough to
settle the numeric presentation of completion, the sheet layout, or the feed /
confirm queue / lots surfaces. **Do not infer those from the prototypes.**

## Process note for the next session

The prototypes were generated by parallel subagents and their descriptions were
relayed to the owner **without being viewed first**. A later browser check found
the real defects: no `<meta name="viewport">` in any of them (so they would have
rendered at 980px on an actual iPhone), literal `undefined` strings in one
prototype's tables, 8px type, and mouse-sized tap targets.

**Verify generated artifacts before presenting them.** Grepping CSS is not
verification — two of the three problems I first suspected were wrong, and the
one that mattered was invisible to grep.
